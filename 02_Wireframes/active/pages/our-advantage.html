Intent: show why embedded payments are better.

Payments should feel invisible — the value is how deeply they are embedded in the software each business already runs, compared with a processor bolted on beside it. One team owns it when something breaks, and the payment is part of the workflow the customer is already in.

Embedded in the software

  • One system of record: the payment, the invoice, and the customer live in the same place.
  • One team to call when something goes wrong.
  • Priced inside the software the business already pays for.
  • The industry's own workflow — deposits, tabs, retainers — not a generic checkout.

Bolted on beside it

  • The payment lives in a second system that has to be reconciled by hand.
  • Processor and software vendor point at each other when it breaks.
  • Another contract, another statement, another support queue.
  • A generic checkout that the industry has to work around.

FAQ

Intent: answer common questions about Our Advantage.

Are Payments, Lending, and Insurance separate acquisitions?

No. They are embedded capabilities added to the software businesses we own, helping them grow without changing their core focus.

What about AI?

AI at Fullsteam focuses on practical use cases that leverage proprietary vertical data — applied where it helps the business, not as a standalone replacement story.

Do these replace what a vertical does?

No. They extend the system of record the industry already depends on.
