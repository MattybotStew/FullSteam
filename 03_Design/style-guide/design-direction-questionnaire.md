> Note: the "direction" and its palette are the form's *own* algorithm output from the

### Project
- **Audience:** Software founders and sellers (owners of vertical software companies), and
  investors — large private-equity firms and sovereign wealth funds. Secondary audiences:
  customers of Fullsteam's vertical software companies, and employees/candidates. Primary
  lens: investors, founders secondary.
- **Audience comfort with technology:** 3 of 5 (Confident)
- **Primary action:** Explore our vertical solutions, then take the next step and start a
  conversation with Fullsteam (Contact). The site is a credibility/validation destination,
  not a lead-gen funnel — its core job is helping a warm visitor understand who Fullsteam
  is before a meeting.
- **Success looks like:** A founder or investor leaves understanding Fullsteam's scale and
  its two-part model — we acquire and grow vertical software companies, and we grow them
  through Our Advantage (payments, lending, insurance, AI) — and reaches out. Success
  is measured by exploration depth and credibility, not form fills.

### Brand
- **Guidelines:** We have a logo and some guidance
- **Openness to change:** 2 of 5 (Refine what exists)
- **Accessibility:** Important to us

### Inspiration
- **Inspired by:** Quilt Software (quiltsoftware.com) — brand consistency, a premium feel,
  prominent company stats, and strong use of video (mission video + customer video
  testimonials). Bending Spoons (bendingspoons.com) — a unique, engaging experience with a
  premium product feel on the homepage. Square (squareup.com) — overall look and feel and a
  seamless way of introducing broad verticals. TogetherWork (togetherwork.com) — clean look
  and feel; introducing verticals directly on the homepage without a page per industry.
  DaySmart (daysmart.com) — how they showcase the product portfolio and talk about mission
  and values. Frontier Growth (frontiergrowth.com) — use of video (including hero video)
  and well-structured homepage content blocks (not their font). Investor-brand feel:
  silverlake.com and thomabravo.com. AI presentation: banyansoftware.com/ai-at-banyan and
  ecisolutions.com/ai-strategy.
- **Avoid:** Stock photography; dated typography and look & feel (the Frontier Growth
  font); talking-head-only video and "word walls"; an over-GTM, product-led portfolio
  framing (DaySmart); framing an acquisition as an "exit" (it's "a home for your
  business"); the label "Platform" (use "Our Advantage"); publishing revenue,
  run-rate, or explicit profitability; floating/scattered vertical "bubbles"; and
  PPC/lead-gen funnels.

### Voice
- **A sentence that sounds like us:** "Whatever the industry, we own the software it runs
  on. A permanent home for each software business we buy — plus the Offerings that help it
  grow."
