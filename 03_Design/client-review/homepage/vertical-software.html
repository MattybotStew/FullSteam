Common
questions, answered.

Do you buy software companies, or add services to them?

We buy software businesses. Services do not lead. Software is the system of record, and AI and payments are added on top of it — not in place of it.

Do you change the brand or the team?

No. The companies keep their names, their teams, and their relationship with their customers. What changes is what sits behind them: shared engineering, go-to-market support, and capital.

Is AI part of this strategy?

Yes, as an addition to software that already runs the business — not as the headline. AI is most useful where the system of record is already in place.
