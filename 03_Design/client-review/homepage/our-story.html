2018

Founded with purpose

Fullsteam launched to power vertical software through integrated payments and shared services — six acquisitions, offices in Auburn and New York, and a proprietary payments platform in year one.

2019

Expanding capabilities

Payments went live with Visa PayFac registration and PCI certification; fifteen more companies joined the platform across self-storage, parking, eyecare, and more.

2020

Building resilience

Payments volume doubled while the team scaled — vertical software became essential infrastructure for customers navigating the pandemic.

2021

Breaking new ground

The first international acquisition — Commerce7 in Canada — plus meaningful gains in revenue and transaction volume.

2022

Growth in motion

Fifty-plus acquisitions, more than a thousand colleagues, and a step-change in revenue and payments footprint.

2023

Reaching new heights

Record monthly payment volume, landmark acquisitions including Silverware and Maestro, and a major recapitalization.

2024

Accelerating progress

Eleven acquisitions, strong ARR growth, the first insurance offering, and a unified go-to-market kickoff.

2025

Building for the future

AI-based tools, sales and marketing investment, and new acquisitions — a future-proof home for vertical software to compound.
