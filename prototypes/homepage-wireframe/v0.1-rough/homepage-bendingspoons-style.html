The model — two stories, one engine

We don't build software companies. We build the software businesses run on.

Fullsteam acquires established, vertical-specific software companies and grows them into market leaders — the tools that are the daily system of record for their industry.

Then we do what a standalone software company rarely can: we expand each one with embedded Offerings — payments, lending, insurance, hardware, and integrations — turning a software purchase into a compounding revenue engine.

Here, we acquire to own and operate for the long term.
